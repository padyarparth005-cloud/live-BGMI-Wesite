About BGMI

Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI) is a multiplayer battle royale game developed by KRAFTON for Indian gamers. The game offers realistic graphics, strategic gameplay, immersive maps, and high-level esports competitions.

BGMI became one of India's most downloaded mobile games and created a massive esports ecosystem involving professional players, streamers, content creators, and gaming organizations.

According to KRAFTON India, BGMI has crossed over 180 million downloads and continues to expand with new updates, collaborations, and tournaments.

BGMI Esports

KRAFTON India Esports organizes official national-level tournaments for professional and amateur players. The BGMI esports ecosystem continues to grow rapidly with millions of viewers and professional organizations.

  • BGIS - Battlegrounds Mobile India Series
  • BMPS - Battlegrounds Mobile Pro Series
  • BMIC - Battlegrounds Mobile India Cup
  • Open qualifiers for all Indian teams
  • Massive LAN Finals and live audience events

Official Tournament History
